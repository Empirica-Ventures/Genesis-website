THE GENESIS PROGRAM

A structured, year-long Project-Based Learning implementation for Grades 4 to 8.

Curriculum Aligned SLO Based
01 · WHO WE ARE

Genesis Education Solutions is a 21st-Century skills nurturing partner through structured Project-Based Learning built for Pakistan's schools, classrooms, and teachers.

Not a tutoring centre STEM vendor workshop company

We work inside your existing timetable and teachers, delivering your existing curriculum through a richer process.

Every project is built by educators for real Pakistani contexts, using locally available materials and mapped to curriculum SLOs.

04 · HOW ASSESSMENT WORKS

A capability layer on top of your grading.

Genesis assessment combines qualitative and quantitative measures, evaluated across two parts. The school's formal grading continues unchanged; Genesis adds a capability layer on top of it.

30RUBRIC POINTS

Qualitative assessment

Rubric-based, evaluated by the teacher: a portfolio review of the completed Student Booklet, a brief individual oral check, and a rubric evaluation across process, product, and 21st-century skills.

10MARKS

Quantitative assessment

Based on multiple-choice questions covering content from every subject the project touched, provided by Genesis in the Teacher Guide for the teacher to administer.
